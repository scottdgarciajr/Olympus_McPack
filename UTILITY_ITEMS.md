# Hephaestus utility creations — v3.4.0

Only Hephaestus can forge these. Anyone can trade, store and use them. Hold exactly one fresh, unenchanted, unbound base item and carry the second ingredient plus all listed costs. Use the Divine Forge; the base and one second ingredient are consumed. XP costs are levels.

| Creation | Base + second ingredient | Additional cost | Controls and limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lantern Helmet | iron helmet + lantern | 8 levels, 2 diamond, 8 emerald, 8 lapis lazuli, 8 copper ingot, 4 iron ingot | Wear: level-12 moving light. One coal/charcoal fuels 90 active seconds. Iron-level head protection; no night vision. |
| Clockwork Trackless Cart | minecart + lodestone | 45 levels, 24 diamond, 48 emerald, 48 lapis lazuli, 2 netherite ingot, 64 iron ingot, 32 redstone, 16 gold ingot | Use on clear ground to deploy. Ride and steer; 6.4 blocks/sec, one-block steps, no flight. Coal/charcoal: 45 driving seconds. Sneak-interact while empty to pack; fuel/health are retained. |
| Wayfinder Compass | compass + amethyst shard | 12 levels, 3 diamond, 12 emerald, 16 lapis lazuli, 12 copper ingot, 8 redstone | Sneak-use: save one waypoint. Use: bearing and distance in the same dimension. No teleportation or target detection. |
| Ariadne Trail Spool | lead + echo shard | 24 levels, 8 diamond, 24 emerald, 32 lapis lazuli, 32 string, 16 redstone, 8 gold ingot | Use: toggle breadcrumb recording while held. Sneak-use: retrace the last 48 markers (4-block spacing). No teleportation; trail stays with the item. |
| Mason Builder Wand | diamond shovel + scaffolding | 32 levels, 12 diamond, 32 emerald, 32 lapis lazuli, 1 netherite ingot, 24 iron ingot, 24 redstone, 24 quartz | Use: place up to eight ordinary building blocks in a row beside the aimed face. Put building blocks in the next hotbar slot. Consumes every block; 3-second cooldown; no containers or redstone machinery. |
| Seedweaver Planter | iron hoe + glow berries | 16 levels, 4 diamond, 16 emerald, 16 lapis lazuli, 12 iron ingot, 12 redstone, 16 bone meal | Use on farmland: plant a 3x3 area using wheat seeds, carrots, potatoes or beetroot seeds from the next hotbar slot. Consumes each planting; no growth boost. 5-second cooldown. |
| Autumn Harvest Sickle | iron hoe + shears | 24 levels, 8 diamond, 24 emerald, 24 lapis lazuli, 16 iron ingot, 16 redstone, 8 gold ingot | Use: harvest at most nine ripe crops in a 3x3 area. Replants only if you carry the matching seed/crop. Normal drops; no Fortune bonus or instant growth. 12-second cooldown. |
| Reclaimer Salvage Tool | iron axe + blast furnace | 26 levels, 8 diamond, 24 emerald, 24 lapis lazuli, 24 iron ingot, 16 redstone | Use: choose an unenchanted vanilla iron tool/armor to destroy and recover at most half its remaining iron value. No player, mob, chest or ore detection. 15-second cooldown. |
| Mason Survey Level | clock + smooth stone | 12 levels, 3 diamond, 12 emerald, 12 lapis lazuli, 8 iron ingot, 8 redstone, 8 quartz | Use: preview a square footprint and center line on the ground for eight seconds. Sneak-use: cycle 5/9/13-block widths. Measuring only; never places or breaks terrain. |

Tool durability is consumed per successful activation; repair durable utility tools with iron. Utility particles are private to the user. The helmet has iron-level protection and 165 durability. Cart packing retains remaining fuel and damage; destroying a cart returns an unfueled kit. No utility adds combat damage or god-level combat buffs.
