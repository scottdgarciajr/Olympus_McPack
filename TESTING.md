# In-game test checklist

Run on a backed-up Bedrock test world with both packs active. Check the content log if a step fails. The offline build does not substitute for these tests.

1. Import **`dist/Olympus_SMP_FINAL.mcaddon`** and activate the behavior pack. `/function olympus/status` should say `v1.9.0 script active`. If it reports an older pack, deactivate the old behavior/resource packs and activate version 1.9.0.
2. Run `/function olympus/summon_mountain` in a clear Overworld area and use the sigil. Keep the chunk loaded. Verify a 24-block stepped mountain, temple, central chest with eight relics, and enchanting-table altar beside it. The chest should refill a relic after one is removed without losing unrelated items.
3. Tap the altar with an empty hand. Inspect each god and claim one. Confirm a title, relic in inventory, passives, and `/function olympus/controls`. A second player must not claim the same god. Reset, then use a relic far from Olympus; the role should claim immediately. Test in-air use and use on an ordinary block. The chest and altar paths should still work.
4. Switch equipment, die, leave, and rejoin. Your chosen role and passives should persist. The claimed relic should stay in inventory after death. Poseidon's Tidecaller, Apollo's Solar Bow, and Hephaestus's Divine Fortune pickaxe should stay with their owners after death; use each repeatedly and verify its durability returns to full. Run the reset function as an operator and verify the role is released.
5. **Hermes:** Confirm Speed VI while walking. Hold sprint and check Speed XIV with directional momentum, a bright gold trail, and a speed clearly above the fastest horse available in your test world. Face north and east in turn; left input should move left and right input should move right at turbo speed. Release sprint or sneak and confirm turbo clears. Check Weakness III. Use the relic for one pearl and check its 5-minute cooldown. Sneak-use to switch constant glide off and on. Test a controlled long fall on each setting; no ordinary fall damage should occur. Test again on a laggy server before relying on extreme falls.
6. **Zeus:** Mining Fatigue II stays active. During open rain, Strength IV appears and ends when weather clears. Aim at a block or enemy within 24 blocks and use the relic for lightning. Sneak-use to start flight. In clear weather, each action has an independent three-minute cooldown. In rain/thunder, use each repeatedly without a cooldown, including under cover; flight still lasts 30 seconds per activation. Jump rises, sneak descends, and no input hovers.
7. **Poseidon:** Conduit Power and Dolphin's Grace III appear in water and open rain. Swim and wade in shallow water to verify directional acceleration, correct left/right strafing, and blue trail; speed should be lower than v1.6. During open rain, test the same speed burst on dry land. Sneak-use the relic to turn Tidal Sprint off, confirm the scripted burst and trail stop but passive effects remain, then turn it on again. Check that Poseidon falls at normal speed and avoids fall damage during rain or thunder, then takes normal fall damage in clear weather. Test a short fall first, then a tall fall on a backed-up world. Slowness III/Weakness III appear in desert and Nether. The Riptide III trident works only under normal water/rain restrictions; a full native launch should get an extra push.
8. **Hades:** Fire Resistance and Night Vision persist; Overworld daylight applies Slowness III and Weakness III. Nether and End apply Strength IV and Haste IV. Relic use gives information only.
9. **Ares:** Strength II, Resistance II, and Slowness III are passive. War Rage gives Strength V and Resistance III for 30 seconds and then returns to Strength II/Resistance II. Reuse is blocked for five minutes.
10. **Apollo:** The Infinity bow consumes no normal arrow while one arrow remains in inventory. Its scripted bonus is +6 damage in direct sunlight and +2 elsewhere. Underground/night Weakness III and Mining Fatigue II appear. Direct-daylight relic use starts 30-second flight; leaving sunlight ends it. Check its three-minute cooldown.
11. **Hephaestus:** Haste IV and Weakness III stay active without holding the pickaxe. The pickaxe shows Fortune III, Efficiency V, Unbreaking III, and Divine Fortune IV lore. Mine a sample of eligible ores to check occasional bonus drops. Relic use grants no unrelated combat buff.
12. **Dionysus:** Melee-hit several different mobs quickly. Every hit should trigger: each target receives a debuff, Dionysus receives a buff, Nausea, and a random safe teleport. Several 180-second buffs should coexist. Once all five buff types are present, further hits should raise existing levels up to IV. Hit the same mob twice within three seconds; the second hit should not retrigger, but another mob should trigger immediately. Build toward 10 Madness stacks: Nausea should become much stronger and Blindness should sometimes trigger at higher stacks. Confirm a failed teleport does not place the player inside solid or hazardous blocks. Use Bone Meal repeatedly and check the stack refills. Test PvP if enabled.
13. Have eight players claim different gods. Check effects and cooldowns stay per player through a server restart. Check the unique aura/emblem and sound on each role's claim and activation.
