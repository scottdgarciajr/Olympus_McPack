# In-game test checklist

Run on a backed-up Bedrock test world with both packs active. Check the content log if a step fails. The offline build does not substitute for these tests.

## Crown verification

1. Run `/function olympus/setup` once. At the existing gold center, interact with the sea lantern. Check the Crown trial lists gold, iron, emerald, and lapis. Claim a god first; then test missing offerings and a successful claim consuming one of each. A second player must not be able to claim the Crown.
2. Wear and remove the Crown. Check that non-Zeus god penalties and existing ability cooldowns change only while it is worn. Check Zeus's Mining Fatigue and storm flight/lightning remain unchanged. Apply an unrelated negative effect and confirm it remains. Check Poseidon's Conduit Power, Dolphin's Grace, Tidal Sprint, and Riptide in natural and Crown-created exposed rain without submersion.
3. Use the Seal menu for day, night, clear, rain, and thunder. Check the shared five-minute cooldown, two-minute weather restoration, cooldown persistence after transfer and restart, and Zeus's existing thunderstorm behavior.
4. Kill the holder with melee and a player-shot arrow. Verify ownership and gear transfer. Die to fall damage and verify ownership is retained on respawn. Check disconnect and rejoin, a full inventory, and `/function olympus/crown_recover`.
5. Check the five-minute rounded coordinate reveal and dimension. Wear the Crown outside the Hall for fifteen minutes to earn Glory; repeat with time split across two ownership periods. Check no Glory in the Hall safe zone and that `/function olympus/crown_glory` survives restart and Crown reset.
6. Activate both Dominion modes on ordinary natural terrain. Verify the 12-block zone, 30-second expiry, separate five-minute cooldown, Hades's Underworld synergy, and no transformation of builds. Test a fully enclosed natural stone water/lava basin and an open shoreline; only the safe basin should convert. Verify restoration after expiry, dismissal, Crown transfer, unequip, disconnect, and world restart. Test unloaded chunks returning later and confirm no duplicate drops or lingering temporary blocks.

These steps require a running Bedrock world and have not been verified by the offline build.

1. Import **`dist/Olympus_SMP_FINAL.mcaddon`** and activate both packs. `/function olympus/status` should say `v2.4.2 script active`. If it reports an older pack, deactivate the old behavior/resource packs and activate version 2.4.2.
2. In a backed-up Overworld test world, stand near world spawn and run `/function olympus/setup`. Verify the Hall is 33 × 15 × 33, has a broad central aisle and eight distinct enchanting-table altars. Check paired positions from the northwest structure corner: Hermes (5,5,6), Zeus (27,5,6), Poseidon (5,5,13), Hades (27,5,13), Ares (5,5,20), Apollo (27,5,20), Hephaestus (5,5,27), Dionysus (27,5,27). If no automatic site qualifies, verify the Hall Placement Seal appears; use it in a backed-up test world at a chosen site and confirm the full 33 × 15 × 33 volume overwrites obstacles. Test placing in another dimension and interacting with its altars and Crown throne. Confirm a second setup refuses to overwrite the Hall. Place a player build in a candidate site in a separate test world and confirm setup skips it.
3. Tap each altar with an empty hand. Verify its ability, weakness, offering and claim state. Reserve Hermes; a second player must be refused during the 10-minute window. Return with 8 feathers, claim, and check that exactly 8 are removed and the role/relic granted. Check missing items, expired reservations, and one reservation per player. A second player must not claim the same god. An unclaimed player using a loose relic must be directed to the altar. Repeat the offering flow for all eight gods.
4. Optionally run `/function olympus/summon_mountain` in a clear area and use the sigil. Its old summit altar is informational; it must no longer bypass offerings.
5. Switch equipment, die, leave, and rejoin. Your chosen role and passives should persist. The claimed relic should stay in inventory after death. Poseidon's Tidecaller, Apollo's Solar Bow, and Hephaestus's Divine Fortune pickaxe should stay with their owners after death; use each repeatedly and verify its durability returns to full. Run the reset function as an operator and verify the role is released.
6. **Hermes:** Confirm Speed VI while walking. Hold sprint and check Speed XIV with directional momentum and the bright gold trail. Check Weakness III and the pearl's five-minute cooldown. Sneak-use to open the mobility menu and toggle Slow Falling and Divine Leap independently. Leap starts OFF; ON should clear about two blocks without midair double-jump or flight, then OFF should restore normal jumping within one second. Check persistence across death, dimension change, and restart. Apply an external Jump Boost potion, toggle Divine Leap OFF, and confirm the potion remains. Retest existing fall protection.
7. **Zeus:** Mining Fatigue II stays active. Confirm no Zeus-specific Strength in clear, rain, or thunder. Aim at a block or enemy within 24 blocks and use the relic for lightning. In thunder, a second strike within five seconds must fail; wait five seconds and retry. Switch weather, remove and re-equip gear, reconnect, and transfer the Crown during the interval to ensure none reset it. Clear weather keeps the three-minute lightning and flight cooldowns. Flight remains unrestricted in rain/thunder and lasts 30 seconds per activation.
8. **Poseidon:** Strength II appears only when actually swimming or head-submerged; shallow foot contact must not grant it. Exposed rain/thunder gives Strength I on dry land, none on clear dry land. Conduit Power, Dolphin's Grace III, Tidal Sprint, Riptide, and fall protection must still work in exposed rain without submersion, including Crown-created rain. Test desert and Nether weaknesses and directional acceleration.
9. **Hades:** Fire Resistance and Night Vision persist. Overworld night gives Strength I/Haste I, including Crown-created night. Daylight debuffs apply in an open ravine but clear in a cave and under a building roof. Daytime shelter does not grant nighttime buffs. Nether and End retain Strength IV/Haste IV. Underworld Dominion grants those realm buffs only inside its zone. Use the relic while standing in regular fire, soul fire, and lava: a visible fireball should follow the crosshair, deal about 6 health points before reductions, and respect a 15-second cooldown (7.5 seconds crowned). Test ordinary ground, nearby lava without touching it, Nether without fire, netherrack, and Dominion without fire; all must refuse. Shoot a forest and player build: no ignition or block damage. Check cooldown after death, dimension travel, relog, and Crown transfer.
10. **Ares:** Strength II, Resistance II, and Slowness III are passive. War Rage gives Strength V and Resistance III for 30 seconds and then returns to Strength II/Resistance II. Reuse is blocked for five minutes.
11. **Apollo:** The Infinity bow consumes no normal arrow while one arrow remains in inventory. Its scripted bonus is +6 damage in direct sunlight and +2 elsewhere. Underground/night Weakness III and Mining Fatigue II appear. Use the relic menu for 30-second Sunflight; leaving sunlight ends it and its three-minute cooldown remains. Arm Solar Flare, fire the divine bow at both a block and a mob, and confirm only that arrow bursts. Check a bright flying beam, sun shaft, two impact rings, three-second Blindness, a 3-block radius, at most 4 added damage to nearby targets, no second area hit on the direct target, no block/fire changes, and 45-second cooldown (about 22.5 crowned). Try other bows, other gods, missed arrows, and reconnecting. Activate Oracle's Sight with players, animals, and mobs at 10/40/41 blocks and in another dimension: only current-dimension targets within 40 should receive viewer-only colored target halos, a local particle bearing ray, and directional hints for eight seconds, without exact coordinates. Test no-player feedback, spectators, 120-second cooldown (60 crowned), and no lingering reveal after expiry.
12. **Hephaestus:** Haste IV and Weakness III remain, and Divine Fortune still gives occasional bonus ore drops. Use the Hephaestus relic to read all four recipes and exact costs. Craft a portable Divine Forge with eight iron surrounding one crafting table, place it outside the Hall, and open its smithing-table menu. Also test the Hall smithing table beside his altar. Browse every recipe while holding the wrong base item, then hold the correct base item to combine. Verify ingredient and configured material/XP deductions, refusal on insufficient resources, single combination per item, retained base enchantments/durability, and actual effects after trade, death, and restart. Verify the Sword-Pickaxe adds only one damage, the axe ignites for only three seconds, and no combined item edits terrain or kills a normally equipped player in one hit. Other gods should see the forge information but be unable to place a portable forge or combine items. Crown ownership must not change forge access.
13. **Dionysus:** Melee-hit several different mobs quickly. Every hit should trigger: each target receives a debuff, Dionysus receives a buff, Nausea, and an 8–16 block teleport onto a clear floor. Check open ground, a cave, a roof, water/lava, cactus and magma; unsafe destinations must be rejected and the player must get feedback if no safe spot exists. The five buff types should coexist. Regeneration must cap at II and last 60 seconds per roll; Resistance must cap at III, while Strength, Speed, and Haste may rise to IV for 180 seconds. Repeated hits should refresh those durations without raising capped levels. Try standing in lava with both god buffs fully built: without a separate Fire Resistance effect, his health should fall rather than remain indefinitely full. Check that a stronger potion or beacon effect is not replaced by a weaker god roll. Hit the same mob twice within three seconds; the second hit should not retrigger, but another mob should trigger immediately. Build toward 10 Madness stacks and verify Nausea and Blindness. Use a single Bone Meal on crops, saplings, grass, and flowers in Survival; successful uses should restore exactly one and follow normal growth behavior. Move the item to a chest and confirm it does not magically refill. A non-Dionysus player must consume Bone Meal normally. Lose the last item and use the relic for one replacement; repeated use within five minutes must not create more. Test combat and harvest without the Crown.
14. Have eight players claim different gods. Check effects and cooldowns stay per player through a server restart. Check the unique aura/emblem and sound on each role's claim and activation.

15. **Hall protection and Crown reconciliation:** Try mining and placing blocks in the Hall as a player and detonating TNT beside it; Hall blocks should remain. Test the portable forge outside the Hall, breaking it to recover its item, then placing it elsewhere. Claim and equip the Crown, disconnect/rejoin, and activate Dimensional Dominion; Crown Status should show worn. If the owner is a different player, Dominion must refuse.
