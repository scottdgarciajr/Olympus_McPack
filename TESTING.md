# In-game test checklist

Run on a backed-up Bedrock test world with both packs active. Check the content log if a step fails. The offline build does not substitute for these tests.

## Crown verification

1. Run `/function olympus/setup` once. At the gold center, interact with the sea lantern. Check the eight offerings: Ender Pearl, Creeper Head, Prismarine Crystals, Blaze Rod, War Trophy, Ominous Bottle, Netherite Ingot, and Poisonous Potato. Confirm Zeus lightning can charge a creeper for the head hunt, Poseidon can fight guardians underwater, Apollo can hunt an outpost captain in daylight, and Dionysus can rapidly grow potatoes with replenished Bone Meal for the rare poisonous drop. Claim a god, then test missing offerings and a successful claim consuming one of each. Confirm a PvP kill awards the custom War Trophy.
2. Wear and remove the Crown. Check that non-Zeus god penalties and existing ability cooldowns change only while it is worn. Check Zeus's Mining Fatigue and storm flight/lightning remain unchanged. Apply an unrelated negative effect and confirm it remains. Check Poseidon's Conduit Power, Dolphin's Grace, Tidal Sprint, and Riptide in natural and Crown-created exposed rain without submersion.
3. Use the Seal menu for day, night, clear, rain, and thunder. Check each Crown's five-minute Authority cooldown, two-minute weather restoration, cooldown persistence after transfer and restart, and Zeus's existing thunderstorm behavior.
4. Kill the holder with melee and a player-shot arrow. Verify ownership and gear transfer. Die to fall damage and verify ownership is retained on respawn. Check disconnect and rejoin, a full inventory, and `/function olympus/crown_recover`.
5. Check the five-minute rounded coordinate reveal and dimension. Wear the Crown outside the Hall for fifteen minutes to earn Glory; repeat with time split across two ownership periods. Check no Glory in the Hall safe zone and that `/function olympus/crown_glory` survives restart and Crown reset.
6. Activate both Dominion modes on ordinary natural terrain. Verify the 12-block zone, 30-second expiry, five-minute cooldown per Crown, Hades's Underworld synergy, and no transformation of builds. Test water and lava at player height and up to three blocks above or below, including an open shoreline. Verify restoration after expiry, dismissal, Crown transfer, unequip, disconnect, and world restart. Fluid flow or mixing can change neighboring blocks outside the restoration record. Test unloaded chunks returning later and confirm no duplicate drops or lingering temporary blocks.
7. Defeat the Ender Dragon in the End, then bring one Dragon Egg and a new set of all eight offerings to the throne. Try forging without wearing the existing Crown: it must refuse without consuming supplies. Have the current owner wear their own Crown and select an online claimed recipient. Verify the owner keeps the original Crown and all its powers, the recipient gets a separate fully powered Crown, the egg and offerings are consumed, and exactly one recorded victory is spent. Extra vanilla eggs must not allow a second duplicate after that one kill. Respawn and kill the dragon again, confirm another egg is awarded, then forge a third Crown with a fresh offering set. Verify separate cooldowns, independent PvP transfer, and recovery after rejoining. There should be no two-Crown cap.

These steps require a running Bedrock world and have not been verified by the offline build.

1. Import **`dist/Olympus_SMP_FINAL.mcaddon`** and activate both packs. `/function olympus/status` should say `v2.7.3 script active`. If it reports an older pack, deactivate the old behavior/resource packs and activate version 2.7.3.
2. In a backed-up Overworld test world, run `/function olympus/setup`. Verify the existing 33 × 15 × 33 Hall retains its eight enchanting-table altars and gains a dark lower chamber seven blocks below the original origin. Descend the rear-center scaffolding and interact with the netherite block at Hall offset (16,-5,23) for Kratos. On an older world, stand inside the loaded Hall and run setup again; the upper Hall should stay put while the lower chamber is added once. Test the placement seal in a separate backed-up world and confirm the lower chamber is also placed.
3. Tap each altar with an empty hand. Verify its ability, weakness, offering and claim state. Reserve Hermes; a second player must be refused during the 10-minute window. Return with 8 feathers, claim, and check that exactly 8 are removed and the role/relic granted. Repeat for all nine gods; Kratos requires an Iron Sword. An operator can run `/scriptevent olympus:open_god hermes` and let a second player claim Hermes without affecting the first. Test `/scriptevent olympus:release_god hermes` separately to vacate all Hermes claims and revoke former owners on return.
4. Optionally run `/function olympus/summon_mountain` in a clear area and use the sigil. Its old summit altar is informational; it must no longer bypass offerings.
5. Switch equipment, die, leave, and rejoin. Your chosen role and passives should persist. The claimed relic should stay in inventory after death. Poseidon's Tidecaller, Apollo's Solar Bow, and Hephaestus's Divine Fortune pickaxe should stay with their owners after death; use each repeatedly and verify its durability returns to full. Run the reset function as an operator and verify the role is released.
6. **Hermes:** Confirm Speed VI while walking. Hold sprint and check Speed XIV with directional momentum and the bright gold trail. Check Weakness III and the pearl's five-minute cooldown. Sneak-use to open the mobility menu and toggle Slow Falling and Divine Leap independently. Leap starts OFF; ON should clear about two blocks without midair double-jump or flight, then OFF should restore normal jumping within one second. Check persistence across death, dimension change, and restart. Apply an external Jump Boost potion, toggle Divine Leap OFF, and confirm the potion remains. Retest existing fall protection.
7. **Zeus:** Mining Fatigue II stays active. Confirm no Zeus-specific Strength in clear, rain, or thunder. Aim at a block or enemy within 24 blocks and use the relic for lightning. In thunder, a second strike within five seconds must fail; wait five seconds and retry. Switch weather, remove and re-equip gear, reconnect, and transfer the Crown during the interval to ensure none reset it. Clear weather keeps the three-minute lightning and flight cooldowns. Flight remains unrestricted in rain/thunder and lasts 30 seconds per activation.
8. **Poseidon:** Strength II appears only when actually swimming or head-submerged; shallow foot contact must not grant it. Exposed rain/thunder gives Strength I on dry land, none on clear dry land. Conduit Power, Dolphin's Grace III, Tidal Sprint, Riptide, and fall protection must still work in exposed rain without submersion, including Crown-created rain. Test desert and Nether weaknesses and directional acceleration.
9. **Hades:** Fire Resistance and Night Vision persist. Overworld night gives Strength I/Haste I, including Crown-created night. Daylight debuffs apply in an open ravine but clear in a cave and under a building roof. Nether and End retain Strength IV/Haste IV. Use the relic while standing in regular fire, soul fire, and lava. Verify a visible ghast-style projectile, native impact damage, and a power-one explosion that ignites and breaks blocks when mob griefing is enabled. Check the 15-second cooldown (7.5 seconds crowned). Ordinary ground and nearby lava without touching it must refuse. Fire the projectile near the Hall; its blocks should remain protected.
10. **Ares:** Strength II, Resistance II, and Slowness III are passive. War Rage gives Strength V and Resistance III for 30 seconds and then returns to Strength II/Resistance II. Reuse is blocked for five minutes.
11. **Apollo:** The Infinity bow consumes no normal arrow while one arrow remains in inventory. Its scripted bonus is +6 damage in direct sunlight and +2 elsewhere. Underground/night Weakness III and Mining Fatigue II appear. Use the relic menu for 30-second Sunflight; leaving sunlight ends it and its three-minute cooldown remains. Arm Solar Flare, fire the divine bow at both a block and a mob, and confirm only that arrow bursts. Check a bright flying beam, sun shaft, two impact rings, three-second Blindness, a 3-block radius, at most 4 added damage to nearby targets, no second area hit on the direct target, no block/fire changes, and 45-second cooldown (about 22.5 crowned). Try other bows, other gods, missed arrows, and reconnecting. Activate Oracle's Sight with players, animals, and mobs at 10/40/41 blocks and in another dimension: only current-dimension targets within 40 should receive viewer-only colored target halos, a local particle bearing ray, and directional hints for eight seconds, without exact coordinates. Test no-player feedback, spectators, 120-second cooldown (60 crowned), and no lingering reveal after expiry.
12. **Hephaestus:** Haste IV and Weakness III remain; Divine Fortune still gives occasional bonus ore drops. Use the relic to read all four recipes, controls, cooldowns, and costs. Craft a portable Divine Forge with eight iron around one crafting table. Check ingredient, material, and XP deductions, and refusal on shortages. Forgecleaver mines ore and has a bounded ore pulse; Cinderbolt fires a ghast-style projectile with a power-one blast that can ignite and break blocks; Undertow Boots render as boots only, grant Dolphin’s Grace I in water, and surge while sneaking; Magnetwood Axe pulls at most 12 dropped item stacks within 8 blocks. Check after trading, death, restart, and while Hephaestus is offline. Non-Hephaestus players may use the products but cannot forge them. Check that no tool kills an armored god in one hit.
    In Creative, search Items for **Hephaestus Creative Test Kit**. Use it with no god assigned and Hephaestus unclaimed: it should claim him and give the relic, enchanted Divine Fortune pickaxe, portable forge, and four finished custom items. Test an existing non-Hephaestus god, and with Hephaestus already claimed by someone else: the kit should not change assignments or grant forge access. Switch to Survival and use another kit; it must grant nothing.
13. **Dionysus:** Melee-hit several different mobs quickly. Every hit should trigger: each target receives a debuff, Dionysus receives a buff, Nausea, and an 8–16 block teleport onto a clear floor. Check open ground, a cave, a roof, water/lava, cactus and magma; unsafe destinations must be rejected and the player must get feedback if no safe spot exists. The five buff types should coexist. Regeneration must cap at II and last 60 seconds per roll; Resistance must cap at III, while Strength, Speed, and Haste may rise to IV for 180 seconds. Repeated hits should refresh those durations without raising capped levels. Try standing in lava with both god buffs fully built: without a separate Fire Resistance effect, his health should fall rather than remain indefinitely full. Check that a stronger potion or beacon effect is not replaced by a weaker god roll. Hit the same mob twice within three seconds; the second hit should not retrigger, but another mob should trigger immediately. Build toward 10 Madness stacks and verify Nausea and Blindness. Use a single Bone Meal on crops, saplings, grass, and flowers in Survival; successful uses should restore exactly one and follow normal growth behavior. Move the item to a chest and confirm it does not magically refill. A non-Dionysus player must consume Bone Meal normally. Lose the last item and use the relic for one replacement; repeated use within five minutes must not create more. Test combat and harvest without the Crown.
14. **Kratos:** Claim at the lower altar with one Iron Sword. Melee-hit each god and confirm one random representative gift or burden lasts eight seconds. If Zeus lightning is copied, use the relic to strike a valid target and verify its 5-second thunder/3-minute clear cooldown. Kill a god and confirm exactly one random copy lasts until Kratos dies; another kill adds exactly one more, with no copy cap and possible additional weaknesses. Verify death clears all lasting and temporary copies and reconnecting does not prematurely clear them. Check that he has no free Strength, that different copied effects coexist, and that duplicate effect levels do not add.

15. Have nine players claim different gods. Check effects and cooldowns stay per player through a server restart. Check the unique aura/emblem and sound on each role's claim and activation. In Creative, use the Olympus Creative Test Kit to switch roles and receive Crown trial offerings, a Dragon Egg, and one test dragon victory; the kit must refuse in Survival.

16. **Hall protection and Crown reconciliation:** Try mining and placing blocks in both Hall levels as a player and detonating TNT beside them; Hall blocks should remain. Test the portable forge outside the Hall, breaking it to recover its item, then placing it elsewhere. Claim and equip the Crown, disconnect/rejoin, and activate Dimensional Dominion; Crown Status should show worn. If the owner is a different player, Dominion must refuse.
